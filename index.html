About

Third year in.
Fully committed.

Anushri Kuity is a third-year BA LLB student, building the foundations of a legal career grounded in rigour, clarity, and genuine intellectual curiosity.

Three years into a five-year integrated degree, she has moved through the foundational pillars of Indian law with steadily growing depth, from constitutional essentials to the procedural mechanics of civil and criminal courts. The classroom, for Anushri, is only the beginning.

Her interests span civil litigation, criminal law, and the emerging frontiers of corporate and family law. She approaches each subject with the same conviction: that precision in law is not just a professional virtue. It is a civic responsibility.
